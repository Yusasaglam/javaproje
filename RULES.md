\- Interface names: Prefix with I, Turkish noun (e.g. IRiskHesaplanabilir)

\- Abstract classes: Turkish noun, no prefix/suffix

\- Constants: UPPER_SNAKE_CASE, Turkish (e.g. MAKS_MIKTAR, MIN_BAKIYE)

\- Do NOT rename existing files unless explicitly asked

\- Apply Turkish names only to new classes, methods, and variables going forward
